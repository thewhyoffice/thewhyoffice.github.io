Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers this website and Why Office Uploader, a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Why Office.

Why Office Uploader

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, captions and cover images to The Why Office channel and to read back the settings of those videos. It is used only by the channel's owner, on the owner's own computer, signed in to the owner's own Google account. No other person can sign in to it.

The tool does not collect, store or share personal information about viewers or any other person. The only data it handles is the channel's own videos and their settings. The sign-in token is stored on the owner's computer and is not shared with anyone.

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms and does not collect personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses as described in its own privacy statement.
